# EngiloMalayalam: what-if ideas

## Owner's list (25 Nov)
1. Travancore, Part 2: Travancore today. Its own currency, army, thorium economy, and Vizhinjam as a national port. Pays off the Part 1 cliffhanger. **(in progress: content/malayalam/travancore-part2/)**
2. What if Travancore had lost the Battle of Colachel (1741)? Dutch rule in Kerala.
3. What if Tipu Sultan had broken the Travancore Lines (1789)? The wall that stopped an empire.
4. What if the Kunjali Marakkars had beaten the Portuguese navy? The Zamorin's Kerala becomes a sea power.
5. What if the 1341 flood hadn't buried Muziris? Kerala's lost port stays a world trade hub.
6. What if Kerala had never united in 1956? Travancore-Cochin and Malabar as separate states.
7. What if the Mullaperiyar 999-year lease (1886) had never been signed?
8. What if Vasco da Gama had been turned away at Kappad (1498)?
9. What if the Gulf boom never happened? Kerala without Gulf money.
10. What if the British had never left? Told only through Kerala, with no maps of India.
