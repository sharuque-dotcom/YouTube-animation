# Travancore what-if: final script for the voiceover (about 55–60 s)

Record **one file per line**, named exactly as below (wav, mp3 or m4a). Keep each line's meaning and order so the animation stays in sync. Wording can change freely; the video re-times itself to the audio.
Delivery: lively storyteller. Line 1 punchy, line 3 three quick beats, a dramatic drop on line 5, curious on 7, a question on 9.

Facts (sources in script.md): independence announcement June 1947 (most sources: 18 June; a few say 11 June); attack on the Dewan by K.C.S. Mani on 25 July 1947 after a concert; Travancore agreed to accede about 30 July 1947. Lines 7–9 are clearly imagination.
