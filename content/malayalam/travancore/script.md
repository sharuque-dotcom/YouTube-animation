# Malayalam What-If Short #1: "What if Travancore became its own country?"

9:16 · about 60 s · English draft for approval (Malayalam version follows, to be checked by the owner) · facts as of 18 Nov 2026
Rules: Shorts only. No Kashmir or Arunachal. No India-wide border maps: the maps show only the Kerala coast and Travancore. Clearly a thought experiment, not a call for anything. Rich cinematic environments and objects, no puppet characters (people only as distant silhouettes).

---

**HOOK (0–4 s)**
In 1947, one kingdom in Kerala almost said no to India.
*A royal conch emblem slams onto a 1947 map of the Travancore coast; the border glows gold.*

**THE REAL STORY**
On the 18th of June, 1947, Travancore announced on the radio that it would become an independent country.
*Night, rain: a valve radio glowing in a dark room; its dial needle moves; sound waves ripple out over the palm-lined coast.*

It already had its own currency, its own stamps, and its own army.
*Three objects drop in, rim-lit: a Travancore coin, an "Anchal" stamp, a soldier's brass badge.*

And its beaches held thorium sand, a nuclear fuel the world wanted.
*A black-sand beach at dusk; the sand glints; a glowing atom traces above it.*

Then, on July 25th, the man behind the plan, Dewan C.P. Ramaswami Iyer, was attacked after a concert in Trivandrum.
*A concert hall at night, a car door, a flash of steel in the dark (no gore); the radio dial snaps back.*

Within days, Travancore agreed to join India.
*The gold border pulses once and merges into the wider coast.*

**THE WHAT-IF**
But imagine if the plan had worked.
*Time-lapse: the map unfreezes, years tick 1947 → 2026.*

A Travancore passport. Embassies in Trivandrum. Ships from Kollam and Alappuzha flying the conch flag.
*A passport stamped with the conch; flags on a boulevard; a harbour at sunrise.*

Would it be a rich little kingdom, like Singapore, or a small state squeezed between giants?
*The coast splits into two futures, gleaming towers vs. a shuttered port.*

Part two: what would Travancore look like today? Follow, so you don't miss it.
*The conch flag ripples; Follow button.*
