# Ricks-style kit: where EngiloMalayalam ep 1 (Travancore) fell short (25 Nov review)

Measured with `pipeline/qa_motion.py` (our own measurement script, run with the cb venv's python):

| | Ours | Kit |
|---|---|---|
| Frames with something moving | 95.4% | 96% or more |
| Longest moment where nothing moves | 1.0 s | 0.5 s or less |
| Share of the picture that's moving | 14% | 30% or more |
| New composition every | 3.3 s | 2.2 s or less |

Fix list for every new Short:
1. Tell a story, not a history lesson. Structure: a hook that sets up, makes people curious and flips; the hero mocked in short quoted lines; time markers; a "wait…?" beat; the twist; one everyday comparison; a closing image that calls back to the hook. A light joke about every 10 s. No spoken call to action: Subscribe and Part 2 are on-screen cards.
2. More of the picture moving: crowds, birds, waves, smoke, and objects passing close to the lens on every shot.
3. Two or three camera beats per line. Rotate the transitions: pull-back from a close-up, follow the object, push-through, foreground wipe, orbit, look-through iris.
4. Change faces every 0.3–0.8 s; eyes dart; mouths move on quoted lines.
5. Real sound effects (about 8 per 10 s, about 12 dB under the voice), a fresh music track each video (about 20 dB under), master to -14 LUFS.
6. Act out the comparison as its own designed character and set.
7. End on the biggest visual moment, then a reaction or an ironic sign, then cut 0.3 s after the last word.
8. Detail pass on every set: clutter, life, ground texture, haze.
9. Every effect is 3D (no flat 2D overlays except the telescope iris).
Kept on purpose: captions at the top and brown skin tones (owner's choices).
