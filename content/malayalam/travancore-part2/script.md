## English (for checking)
1. If Travancore were still a country today, Malayalis wouldn't go to the Gulf... the Gulf would come here looking for jobs.
2. Why? Look at this black sand on the beach. One of the world's largest thorium reserves.
3. Its own army: the Nair Brigade. Its own coin: the chakram. 28 chakrams make one rupee.
4. People would laugh: "Can you buy an iPhone with chakrams?" You can... if you turn up with a sack full of them!
5. But Travancore's secret weapon is something else: Vizhinjam. Just 10 nautical miles from one of the world's busiest shipping lanes.
6. First one ship. Then ten. Then a hundred.
7. Wait... is all this just a dream?
8. No. Vizhinjam port really is working today. Even the world's biggest container ship has docked here.
9. Like a national highway running right past your front yard.
10. Travancore's old dream now sails out to sea, under India's flag.
11. Whether the Gulf will come here, nobody knows... but the ships have started arriving.

## Facts to check before publishing
- Travancore rupee = 28 chakrams (Travancore coinage; Wikipedia "Travancore rupee").
- Nair Brigade = Travancore's army; merged into the Indian Army after 1949.
- India's monazite beach sands (Kerala coast, e.g. Chavara) hold one of the world's largest thorium reserves (IREL / DAE).
- Vizhinjam International Seaport: about 10 nautical miles from the international East–West shipping route; commissioned May 2025; described as India's first deep-water transshipment port.
- MSC Irina (among the world's largest container ships) berthed at Vizhinjam in June 2025. Confirm the date and ship before publishing.
- Lines 1, 4 and 10–11 are clearly imagination ("what if"), not claims.
