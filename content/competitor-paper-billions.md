- "Why the Stock Market Just Crashed": 747k views
- "How a Chinese Spy Got Inside Nvidia": 83k in 11 hours

Seen from YouTube's auto-frames (at 25/50/75% of each video) and the thumbnails.
Watch pages and downloads were blocked, so these notes don't cover pacing, voice or text use.

1. **Characters carry the story.** Round-headed cartoon people in full, warm scenes (a candle-lit dinner, a street, a server room). The real people are caricatures, recognisable from signature features: Jensen's grey hair, glasses and leather jacket; Alice in sunglasses and a red dress. Faces show clear emotion.
2. **Analogies explain the money.** A lemonade stand explains markets and pricing. Companies become characters: an Apple on a throne, Nvidia chips holding hands. The Capitol building has a face.
3. **Scenes over infographics.** Most frames are a scene with characters acting. Text is rare and punchy: a "SALE" sign, "NO" stamps, a calendar.
4. **Small jokes and Easter eggs.** A cat asleep in the server room, a "Retiring in spring" cake.
5. **Thumbnails** show a caricature face with a strong emotion (fear, sweat, love), one big money number in red and white, and a dramatic setting (fire, a cliff edge, candle-light).
6. **Titles** are broad curiosity questions ("How…", "Why…") on the week's biggest topic. Videos run 14–20 minutes. Uploads are fast and topical.
