# Short: "From Dishwasher to $5 Trillion… to Nothing" (Cinderella, part 1)

Toppled Giants Short · 9:16 · about 70 s · DRAFT for approval · facts as of 14 Nov 2026
Trope: Cinderella (rags → riches → midnight). Three acts, each with its own grounded look (no all-over purple dream theme):
- **Act 1 · Humble** (0–25 s): warm film-photo look, 1970s grain, sepia and tungsten light.
- **Act 2 · Rise** (25–45 s): bright and clean, Nvidia green, stage lights, gold.
- **Act 3 · Midnight** (45–70 s): harsh tabloid look, white camera flashes, black and white with red, newspaper print.

**HOOK (0–6 s)**
He started out scrubbing toilets and washing dishes. He ended up running the most valuable company on Earth. Then he lost it in four days.
*Smash cut: a mop bucket, a dish rack, then a gold "$5,000,000,000,000" counter, then a red "RESIGNED" stamp. Jensen's photo in the middle of each.*

**ACT 1 · Humble**
At nine, his family sent him to America. By mistake, he ended up at a tough reform school in Kentucky, where his job was cleaning the bathrooms.
*A 1970s Polaroid of a brick schoolhouse; a mop squeaks across tiles; the "KENTUCKY" caption is hand-written.*

As a teenager in Oregon, he worked nights at Denny's: dishwasher, busboy, waiter.
*A neon diner sign at night in the rain; a stack of plates; a time clock punches "11:58 PM".*

**ACT 2 · Rise**
In 1993, in a Denny's booth in San Jose, he and two friends planned a chip company. They called it Nvidia.
*A diner booth, coffee cups, a napkin sketch of a chip that glows green and becomes a real chip.*

It nearly ran out of money. Then the AI boom hit.
*The bank balance ticks down to "30 days"; then a green line rockets off the chart.*

In October 2025, Nvidia became the first company in history worth five trillion dollars.
*The gold counter slams "$5 TRILLION"; confetti; a keynote stage with 10,000 phone lights.*

The dishwasher had become the king of AI.
*Jensen's cut-out under a spotlight, the leather jacket glinting.*

**ACT 3 · Midnight**
Then, this November, he admitted an affair. Reports say there were two more. Days later, the board accepted his resignation.
*Camera flashes strobe; newspapers slap down; three masks; a black-and-white photo with a red "RESIGNED" stamp. A clock strikes midnight.*

**CLIFFHANGER**
On December 3rd, Congress wants answers. And no one has named the third woman. Follow for part two.
*The Capitol at night; the blank third mask turns toward the camera. "PART 2" and a Follow button.*
