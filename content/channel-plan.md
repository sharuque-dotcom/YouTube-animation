# Channel Plan

## Concept
Short, dramatic animated stories about **frauds, collapses, heists, and the hidden economics behind money and power**.
One format (animated documentary, 8–15 min, thriller pacing), one audience (curious viewers who like true stories about money).

## Channel name

**Top pick: Paper Empires**
- Fits every topic: fake companies, collapsed giants, fortunes that were never real.
- Visual identity writes itself: paper/document textures, burning ledgers, folding paper cities.
- Handle idea: `@PaperEmpires`

Alternatives:
1. **The Dark Ledger**: leans harder into fraud and crime.
2. **Money Noir**: moody, cinematic, short.
3. **Fallen Giants**: strongest for rise-and-fall stories, weaker for heists/economics.

> Before committing: search YouTube, Instagram and X for the name and the handle to make sure nobody big already uses it.

## Upload rhythm
- 1 long video per week (aim for 2 once the pipeline is fast).
- 2–3 Shorts cut from each long video (the hook + the most shocking reveal).
- Judge the niche after 10 uploads, not 2.
