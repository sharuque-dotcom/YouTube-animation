# Episode 7: The Casanova of Silicon Valley? (Nvidia, Part 3)

Toppled Giants · DRAFT for approval · facts as of Friday 13 November 2026 · about 5 minutes
Angle: Casanova, always as a question. Real people stay faceless; here they appear as Venetian carnival masks. Every claim is attributed: one affair was admitted, and the other two are *reported*.

---

**[cold open]**
Venice, the 1700s. A man named Giacomo Casanova writes thousands of pages about his love affairs, and his name becomes a word.
*Night painterly Venice: a canal, lanterns, a gondola gliding through rain; a quill writing by candlelight.*

Three hundred years later, Silicon Valley is asking whether it had a Casanova of its own.
*The canal's reflection ripples and becomes a glass office tower in Santa Clara.*

In four days, the man who built the world's most valuable chip company admitted one affair, was reported to have had two more, and lost the company he ran for 33 years.
*Four calendar pages flip: Sunday to Wednesday. A leather jacket slides off a chair.*

**[title]** *Toppled Giants sting · Episode 7: The Casanova of Silicon Valley? · Nvidia, Part 3*

---

**[chapter 1 · Three Masks]**
On Sunday, Jensen Huang wrote to Nvidia's staff that he had been unfaithful to his wife. "I have fallen short of that responsibility," he wrote.
*A letter on a dark desk; the line glows.*

That same day, the New York Times reported that US intelligence suspected the woman, Alice Huang, of gathering information for China. The two are not related.
*The first carnival mask lit on a stand; a scanning lens passes over it.*

On Wednesday, the Times reported a second relationship, with Kimberly Powell, Nvidia's vice president of health care, who reported directly to him.
*The second mask lights up; an org-chart line runs from it to the CEO's chair.*

Sources told the paper the pair had been filmed being affectionate at a hotel during Nvidia's big developer conference. Three people said his security team approached the person who recorded it and offered a non-disclosure agreement. The Times did not see the footage.
*A hotel corridor at night; a phone screen recording; a security silhouette slides an "NDA" across.*

Then, on Thursday, Reuters reported a third relationship, with another Nvidia employee, citing two people familiar with the matter. She has not been named.
*The third mask lights up, blank and unnamed: "Reuters · 2 sources".*

Nvidia declined to comment on that report. Powell did not respond to requests for comment.
*"No comment" chips settle under the masks.*

---

**[chapter 2 · The Night It Ended]**
On Wednesday night, Nvidia's board met.
*The boardroom from part two, rain on glass, eleven chairs.*

According to a filing with US regulators, the board accepted his resignation as president, chief executive and director, effective immediately.
*An SEC filing page slides in; a stamp: "Form 8-K · effective immediately".*

The Wall Street Journal had reported that directors were preparing to push him out. One congressman called it a firing.
*Two newspaper headlines face each other: "RESIGNED" vs "PUSHED OUT?".*

Chief financial officer Colette Kress became interim CEO, and longtime director Harvey Jones became the board's first-ever chair.
*Two new nameplates slide onto the table; the leather jacket's chair is empty.*

---

**[chapter 3 · Why It Mattered]**
This was never only about romance.
*The masks dim; the spotlight moves to three red flags.*

A boss in a relationship with someone who reports to him raises conflict-of-interest questions.
*Flag one: an org chart with a glowing reporting line.*

A CEO close to a suspected intelligence recruit raises security questions.
*Flag two: a chip under a magnifying lens.*

And shareholders have filed a lawsuit alleging the company should have told investors sooner.
*Flag three: a gavel and a class-action folder.*

The board has said it has no reason to believe national security or company secrets were compromised.
*A green shield, small but steady.*

---

**[chapter 4 · The Fallout]**
On Thursday, Nvidia's shares fell 14 percent at the open, then recovered half the loss and closed down about 4 percent.
*A red candle plunges, then claws back halfway.*

Senator Tim Scott has invited him to a public hearing on December 3rd, and lawmakers have warned of subpoenas.
*The Capitol at night; an invitation envelope stamped "DEC 3".*

Not everyone turned away. Elon Musk replied that hiring him "would be an honor".
*A single chat bubble glows in the dark.*

And on Tuesday, Nvidia reports earnings for the first time in 33 years without him as CEO.
*An empty stage, a spotlight, a "Q3 earnings · Tue" placard.*

---

**[outro]**
Casanova told his story himself, in his memoirs. Jensen Huang has so far said one thing: that he fell short.
*Back to the Venetian canal; the quill stops; the letter from chapter 1 floats on the water.*

The other stories come from anonymous sources, and none of the women has spoken publicly.
*Three masks drift on the canal, unnamed.*

So, Casanova of Silicon Valley? Or a founder who simply got caught? The investigation, and Congress, may tell us more.
*The tower's reflection in the canal goes still. "Still developing · as of 13 November".*
