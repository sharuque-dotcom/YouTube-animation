# Treatment: Episode 2, "The CEO, the Chips and the Alleged Spy"

Toppled Giants · house style: **The Case File** (a light, printed investigation file) · ~7.5 min · narration as recorded

## 1. Three structures, one chosen
1. **Chronological rise → scandal.** Clear but slow; the hook arrives late. No.
2. **Countdown to the board's verdict.** Tense, but there is no verdict yet (the investigation is open). No.
3. **The case file, read exhibit by exhibit.** ✅ Chosen. The film *is* a file: Exhibit A, the email · Exhibit B, the chips · Exhibit C, the company · Exhibit D, the report · Exhibit E, the fallout · and a last page that sorts **Known / Alleged / Unknown**. It suits a developing story: the file ends **open**, which is honest and is the payoff.

## 2. Logline and arc
**The most powerful man in AI admits an affair, and the woman at its centre is reportedly suspected of spying for China. We open the file and sort what is known from what is alleged.**
Setup (the email) → widen (Nvidia, the chip wall) → the link (Megaspeed) → the turn (the report) → fallout (confession, Congress, stock) → ending: the file is stamped **CASE OPEN**, echoing the cold open's first stamp.

## 3. Benchmark (grammar only)
- **Learn from:** newspaper visual-investigation videos (evidence converging, one finding per layout); pop-print case-file explainers (exhibits, stamps, a headline per beat); isometric supply-chain infographics (one world, label pins, a route).
- **Don't take:** any outlet's branding, layouts, fonts, characters or shots.

## 4. The look (house style)
- **Paper:** warm cream page with mottling, a fold and dust. Every frame is printed, including dark beats, which become a dark ink ground with lighter dots.
- **Ink:** deep purple-navy for lines and text. Spot inks: brand purple (overprint shadow), yellow (keywords and numbers), red (stamps and alarms only), teal (money and chips).
- **Shading is halftone dots,** not gradients. Photos of real places and people become **halftone prints** pinned to the file.
- **Type:** black serif headlines with a misregistered purple shadow, chunky numerals, mono case numbers, a caption bar for the key line.
- **Apparatus:** case number top left (`CASE TG-002`), exhibit tab per chapter, REC dot and date top right, stamps.

## 5. Signature shot and native move
- **Signature shot (Exhibit B): the isometric chip world.** A diorama on a floating plinth: Santa Clara HQ block, the Pacific, the US export wall, Singapore and Malaysia data halls, a dashed route towards China with a "?" (alleged). The camera starts on one chip and pulls back to reveal the whole route.
- **Native move:** the **halftone dot wipe.** A grid of dots grows to cover the frame and shrinks onto the next exhibit, and each chapter turns a folder tab.

## 6. Characters and fairness
- Role characters (founders in the diner, investigators, board members) are flat, thick-outlined figures that blink and breathe.
- **Alice Huang: silhouette only, no mugshot, no height chart.** She has not been charged. Stamps are used **only for facts**: ADMITTED (the affair), DENIED (Megaspeed), REPORTED (the intelligence claims), CASE OPEN (the end). No GUILTY, ever.

## 7. Sound
- Music: your four tracks (tension → theme → tension → fall → resolve).
- Foley on every action: paper slide, folder flip, stamp thud, typewriter, marker squeak, pin click, chip clink, dot-wipe whoosh.
- **Two silences:** before "The woman at the centre of it…" and before "On October 29th…". The first sound after each is a single stamp.

## 8. Pacing rules
- Nothing waits for the voice: the first element is on screen within 0.35 s, and numbers start counting at once.
- Text holds at least its reading time (letters ÷ 15 + 1.5 s). There are no blank frames in transitions, and something changes every second.
- At least four camera moves: slow push for scrutiny, snap zoom into halftone for "look closer", pan across the board for connections, pull back for scale.

## 9. Style frames (for approval)
1. **The Case File:** Exhibit A, Jensen Huang's halftone profile with stat callouts and the ADMITTED stamp.
2. **The Evidence Board:** pinned halftone photos, source cards (NYT · WSJ · FT), red marker links to Megaspeed, REPORTED magnets.
3. **The Chip World:** the isometric diorama of the chip route with label pins.
